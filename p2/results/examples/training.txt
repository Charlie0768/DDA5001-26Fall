We are asked to find the **remainder when**
$$
8 \cdot 10^{18} + 1^{18}
$$
**is divided by 9**.

That is, compute:
$$
(8 \cdot 10^{18} + 1^{18}) \mod 9
$$

---

### Step 1: Simplify $1^{18}$
$$
1^{18} = 1
$$
So the expression becomes:
$$
8 \cdot 10^{18} + 1 \mod 9
$$

---

### Step 2: Use modular arithmetic — compute $10^{18} \mod 9$

Note that:
$$
10 \equiv 1 \mod 9
$$
Because $10 - 1 = 9$, which is divisible by 9.

So:
$$
10^{18} \equiv 1^{18} = 1 \mod 9
$$

---

### Step 3: Multiply by 8
$$
8 \cdot 10^{18} \equiv 8 \cdot 1 = 8 \mod 9
$$

---

### Step 4: Add 1
$$
8 \cdot 10^{18} + 1 \equiv 8 + 1 = 9 \mod 9
$$

$$
9 \mod 9 = 0
$$

---

\boxed{0}
